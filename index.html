Meet LaDonna

LaDonna Wright is a Registered Nurse, Relationship Expert, and Entrepreneur. She is the CEO of Shared Sweat LLC and the creator of the best-selling adult game Secret VII!

LaDonna is a devoted wife and proud mother of six. Originally from Madison, Wisconsin, she began her nursing career as an ICU nurse before transitioning into Hospice and Palliative Care, where she supported thousands of patients and families through some of life's most vulnerable moments—with compassion, dignity, and grace.

Through both her professional journey and personal life experiences, LaDonna developed a deep understanding of what truly shapes quality of life. She is passionate about helping people improve their lives through self-care, healthy relationships, and living with purpose—so they can enjoy healthier, happier, and more fulfilling years ahead.

LaDonna is also a committed philanthropist, supporting organizations such as the Prostate Cancer Foundation and the National Alliance on Mental Illness. Known as “The Relationship Nurse,” LaDonna blends clinical expertise, real-life wisdom, and relatable conversations to empower individuals and couples to build stronger relationships—and better lives.
